**Key finding:** The selected structures show **three, two, and one Mn$^{3+}$-like centers** at Na12, Na11 Vac11, and Na10 CL-far, respectively. The detailed oxygen p-projection changes are spatially uneven and dependent on vacancy ordering.

**Boundary:** Neither PDOS-integrated electron populations nor local magnetic moments are formal atomic charges. Na11 Vac11 and Na10 CL-far do not form a nested vacancy-removal sequence.
