# Draft: Mn redox and ligand electronic response upon Na extraction

**Status:** Working Results and Discussion subsection; research checkpoint only. Do not treat as manuscript acceptance, phase-diagram result or final interpretation.

## Results and discussion

For the selected P2-Na$_x$Fe$_{0.5}$Mn$_{0.5}$O$_2$ configurations at $x=0.667$, 0.611 and 0.556, progressive sodium removal is accompanied by a reduction in the count of Mn3+-like centers from three to two and one. The local moments, Mn–O coordination and occupied d-projected spin-polarization magnitudes provide consistent Mn-centered formal-valence fingerprints. Parent Mn22 changes from Mn3+-like to Mn4+-like character by the Na11 Vac11 composition, whereas parent Mn30 undergoes the analogous change in the selected Na10 CL-far state. Parent Mn29 retains a large spin-polarization magnitude but reverses spin direction in the Na10 calculation; this reversal is not evidence for an Mn oxidation event. VASPKIT 1.5.1 and pymatgen yield nine-site d-spin polarizations differing by at most 0.00020 projected electrons, supporting consistency of two postprocessing methods applied to the same underlying VASP calculations.

A corresponding ligand response is seen around the changing Mn22 center. For both Na11 Vac11 and Na11 Vac01, all six Mn22-coordinating oxygen sites have decreased occupied site-projected O p populations compared with the Na12 parent. These six oxygens, representing 16.7% of the oxygen sublattice, contribute 35.80% and 36.37%, respectively, of the sum of absolute oxygen-site projected changes. The correlation between complete 36-site response vectors for the two Na11 configurations is moderate ($r=0.62722$), illustrating appreciable vacancy-ordering sensitivity in the spatial distribution of ligand projections even though the Mn-local formal-valence fingerprint is retained. These orbital-projected populations are not unique atomic electron counts, formal oxidation charges, quantitative oxygen-hole densities or direct covalency metrics.

Of the five Na11 single-vacancy configurations with matched 3×3×2 statics, Vac11 has the lowest calculated energy (-418.24829804 eV per 18-f.u. cell). This selected Na11 state lies 7.2139 meV/f.u. above the tie line joining the sampled Na12 parent and Na10 CL-far endpoints. Although this positive offset indicates a zero-temperature energetic preference of that particular sampled endpoint mixture over Vac11, the full equilibrium stability of the Na11 composition is unresolved because vacancy/magnetic configurations and convergence and entropic contributions have not been exhaustively examined. Moreover, the selected lowest-energy Na11 and Na10 states are not nested Na-removal configurations; the comparison is composition-resolved, not a proven individual extraction pathway.
