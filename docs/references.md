# Research Literature

## Primary motivation

Chakraborty, A., House, R. A., and Islam, M. S. (2025).

Suppressing O-type stacking and cation migration with
Mg and Si doping in P2-type Fe-Mn layered oxides for
sodium-ion cathodes.

Journal of Materials Chemistry A, 13, 17615-17621.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/D5TA00804B

## Relevance to this project

The paper investigates dopant-dependent stability in
P2-type Fe-Mn layered sodium-ion cathodes.

It discusses how Mg and Si influence structural
transformations, cation migration, and oxygen stability.

Si-O bonding provides a motivation for investigating
whether local covalency can contribute to structural
stabilization beyond transition-metal redox capacity.

## Our research extension

Our project proposes a controlled comparison of:

- Mg2+
- Al3+
- Si4+
- Ti4+

The objective is to distinguish effects associated
with redox inventory from those associated with
local metal-oxygen bonding.

Our current completed calculations establish a
P2 parent-structure benchmark.

The proposed dopant-dependent conclusions have
not yet been demonstrated by our calculations.

## Methodological caution

The Oxford paper and our present PBE+U+D3
calculations should not be assumed to use
identical electronic-structure approximations.

Numerical energies obtained using different
functionals are not directly comparable.
